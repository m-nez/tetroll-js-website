How to play

Complete 21 lines first. Do not fill the board vertically 3 times. Your board is on the left.

Controls

rotate blocks clockwise - W, E, Up Arrow, I, O, X
rotate blocks counterclockwise - Q, U, Z
drop - Shift, Space
